# Player's Guide

Your Dungeon Master sent you a GameTable link. Here's everything you need — there's
**no account to make and nothing to install.**

---

## Joining

Open the link your DM gave you (it looks like `https://.../play/xxxxxxxx`) in any
modern browser — on a laptop, tablet, or phone. You're in. Type your **name** in the
side panel so the rest of the table knows who you are.

---

## The map

- You see the map **as your DM has revealed it** — unexplored areas stay dark until
  they uncover them.
- **Pan** by dragging an empty area; **zoom** with the scroll wheel or a pinch.
- **Tokens** are the circles on the map — characters, monsters, and NPCs. They move
  in real time as your DM (or you) move them.
- If your DM has given you a character token, you can **drag your own token** to move
  it. Everything else is theirs to move.

---

## Seeing a creature's info

**Click a token** to open its public card — its picture and whatever description your
DM has made public. (Anything your DM keeps secret simply isn't there for you.)

---

## Handouts

When your DM shows a picture — a map, a letter, a piece of art — a **Handout** button
appears at the top. Switch between **Map** and **Handout** any time; your DM can also
bring the handout up for you.

---

## Rolling dice

In the **Dice** panel:

- Tap a die or type a formula like `1d20`, `2d6+3`, or `1d20+5`, then **Roll**.
- A normal roll shows up in the shared log for the **whole table** to see.
- The **🤫** button rolls **in secret** — only you (and your DM) see the result.

---

## Tips

- If the map looks frozen, your connection may have dropped — it reconnects on its
  own, or just refresh the page; you'll be dropped right back into the current scene.
- Playing on a phone? Turn it sideways for more map.
- The link is your way in — if your DM revokes or replaces it, ask them for the new
  one.

Have fun at the table! 🎲
